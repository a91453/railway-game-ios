### 504. Those still waiting at a station go home when the operating day starts

Owner, 2026-10-30, on 97.1 (a real-map game, day 38): the bubbles along the Sanying line said "full · most wait for Sanying line to Dingpu · longest 2,551 min", about 42 hours. 「每日等待人數應該每日重置」.

**Before.** GameCore had no rule for waiting too long. A station holds 4,000 waiting passengers (`StationPassengers.capacity`); those released into a full station leave at once (`overflowed`). Those already waiting left only by boarding, when their line no longer serves them (`abandonUnservedPassengers`) or when the station closes (`abandonPassengers(waitingAt:)`, decision 65). A queue a line could not clear in a day carried over into the next, and the next, so the longest wait grew without bound.

**The rule.** At 04:00 of every day (`LineAcquisition.sameDayCutoff`, the day boundary a bought line's first service day already uses, decision 490), everyone still waiting at any station goes home, counted as abandoned at their original station: the same count, the same station card line (「放棄搭乘」) and the same station master advice as the other ways of giving up. Riders on board are not touched; a transfer passenger waiting at an interchange is counted at the station they set out from. The minute's release goes on after it, so the new day's first passengers start an empty queue. `GameWorld.advance` does it in the minute steps and in the idle minutes it skips (`sendWaitingPassengersHome(at:)`), so one call and minute-by-minute stay the same world.

No save version: the waiting groups and the `abandoned` count were already saved; a save from before 04:00 simply sends its queue home at the next 04:00.

**Tests.** `PassengerDemandTests.testWaitingPassengersGoHomeWhenTheOperatingDayStarts` (fails on the main before this decision: nobody leaves at 04:00). Tests that ran a station through 04:00 and counted on its queue surviving now count those who went home: `testAFullStationTurnsPassengersAway` (1,000,000 a day into a full station: 4,000 went home at 04:00, so overflowed 992,000, abandoned 4,000), `testPassengersWhoseLineNoLongerTakesThemLeave` (adds those who went home at 04:00 to the counts), `testTheDecoderRejectsPassengersThatBreakTheRules` (the "group from the future" now uses a time after the save's clock: the first group waiting is 04:00's, no longer midnight's, so the old 501 s was simply early).

**Fixtures moved.** See the pull request for the value-by-value account of the golden scenarios and replay checksums this changes.

**Five angles.** Player: the bubbles no longer show waits of tens of hours, a full station gets a fresh start each day, and the problem reads plainly. Railway fan: real stations do not keep people overnight; after the last train the platforms empty. Developer: one deterministic GameCore rule at an existing day boundary, no save version, the existing abandoned count. Art: none. Music: none.

**Next step (not in this decision).** Boarding still takes the farthest-going first (`Boarding.swift`), so short-trip passengers at a busy station can be left behind all day; this decision only stops them from waiting past 04:00. Measured on the owner's save below.
