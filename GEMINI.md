# GEMINI.md

Instructions for Gemini agents (Antigravity, Gemini CLI) in this repository.

Read [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) first and follow it: it is the entry point for
every agent other than Claude Code, says what Antigravity takes in the
division of work, and points to [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), the rule book for
every agent. Then read [`docs/HANDOFF.md`](docs/HANDOFF.md) and claim your
task in the work registry, issue #231, before starting.

Branches start with `gemini/`; commits end with `Agent: Antigravity`.
